Privacy Policy — TATU Attendance Register

Last updated: 28 July 2026

Shreeyog Website and App Designer ("we", "us", "our"), Udyam Reg. No. UDYAM-UK-09-0013602, operates the TATU Attendance Register application ("App"). This page explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and how it is protected.

1. Information We Collect

The App is used by teachers to record and manage classroom attendance. Depending on how the App is used, the following information may be collected and stored:

  • Teacher's name, designation, and login email address
  • Student names and roll numbers entered into attendance registers
  • Attendance records (present/absent status by date, subject, class, and semester)
  • Holiday and leave records entered by the teacher
  • Teacher's photo and signature, if uploaded for use in generated reports
  • Device information required for app functionality (e.g. for offline storage and sync status)

We do not collect location data, contacts, camera/microphone access beyond photo upload, or any information unrelated to attendance record-keeping.

2. How We Use This Information

  • To generate and store attendance registers, leave records, and related PDF reports
  • To sync data across devices via cloud backup, so records are not lost if a device is lost, changed, or reset
  • To restore a teacher's data using their license key on a new device

We do not sell, rent, or share this data with advertisers or third parties. We do not display advertisements in the App.

3. Data Storage and Security

Attendance data is stored locally on the device (using browser storage / IndexedDB) and, for licensed/paid users, additionally synced to Google Firebase Realtime Database for cloud backup. Firebase is a Google Cloud service; data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Access to cloud-stored data is restricted using Firebase Authentication tied to the teacher's login credentials and license key.

4. Data Retention

Data remains stored for as long as the App is in active use under a valid license. Users may request deletion of their cloud-stored data at any time by contacting us using the details below.

5. Children's Data

The App may contain student names as part of classroom attendance records, entered solely by the teacher for institutional record-keeping purposes. This data is not used for any purpose other than attendance management and is not shared publicly or with third parties.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of data associated with your account by contacting us. You may also stop using the App at any time, which will stop further data collection.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
